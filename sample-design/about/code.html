Our Story

Two years on Chalk Lane, grounded in honest craft and good company.

The Old Picture-Framing Shop

Two years ago, we took over what had been a quiet picture-framing workshop for thirty years on the corner of Weaver Street and Chalk Lane. The space still had timber benches and plaster dust behind the baseboards.

We named the place after the seventy-year-old fig tree growing through the flagstones in the rear courtyard. It drops fresh figs every late summer and shades the long bench every morning. We kept the solid timber floors, brought in a drum roaster, built an open bakery counter, and opened our doors to the neighbourhood.

The People Behind the Counter

Mara

Kitchen & Bakery

Mara runs the kitchen and bakes from 5:40 every single morning. She mixes sourdough with a wild levain started five years ago, laminates butter pastries in batches of twenty-four, and pulls hot loaves from the deck ovens just as the front doors unlock. If there is flour on her sleeves, it means everything went according to plan.

Dev

Coffee Roaster

Dev roasts twice a week on the cast-iron machine situated right behind the espresso counter. He sources small-holder beans from high-altitude farms, dials in roast curves batch by batch, and insists on tasting every morning pull before we serve a single cup.

coffee

Our Roasting Schedule

We roast on Tuesday mornings and Friday afternoons. If you visit while the drum is spinning, you'll catch the sweet scent of caramelising coffee beans drifting into the lane. Fresh retail bags of whole bean and custom grinds are restocked right after cooling.
