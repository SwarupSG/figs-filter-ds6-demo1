How to Find Us

On Foot

Turn down Chalk Lane from the high street. Look for our deep purple timber awning and the fig tree in the front courtyard on the corner of Weaver Street.

Public Transit

A 3-minute walk from Weaver Street Station (Lines 2 & 4). Bus routes 14, 27, and 88 stop directly at the corner of Chalk Lane.

Parking

Free 1-hour street parking along Weaver Street. Dedicated bicycle racks are secured right beside our courtyard gate.
